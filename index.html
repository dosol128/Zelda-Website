About the Legend

Created by Shigeru Miyamoto and Takashi Tezuka, The Legend of Zelda is a high-fantasy action-adventure video game franchise created by Nintendo. Since its original release in 1986, it has become one of the most prominent and successful franchises in gaming history.

The series centers on the various incarnations of Link, a courageous young man of the Hylian race, and Princess Zelda, a mortal reincarnation of the goddess Hylia. Together, they fight to save the magical land of Hyrule from Ganon, an evil warlord turned demon king.

Key Achievements

  • Revolutionized the action-adventure genre with open-world exploration.
  • Ocarina of Time (1998) is frequently cited as one of the greatest video games of all time.
  • Introduced battery-backed save features to home consoles.
  • Has sold over 150 million copies worldwide.
